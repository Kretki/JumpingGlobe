Merdith et al. 2021 files NOT used by the globe — safe to delete.

Used (keep):
  meredith.rot                                 rotation model (server: /api/rotations)
  shapes_static_polygons_Merdith_et_al.gpml    cuts land LODs into plates and identifies
                                               the plate of a clicked point (merdith_prep.sh)
  Keep the .gpml file: the API server rebuilds the plate layers from it whenever the
  LODs change.

Not used (delete):
  1000-410-Convergence_Merdith_et_al.gpml      plate-boundary lines (convergent), 1000–410 Ma
  1000-410-Divergence_Merdith_et_al.gpml       plate-boundary lines (divergent), 1000–410 Ma
  1000-410-Transforms_Merdith_et_al.gpml       plate-boundary lines (transform), 1000–410 Ma
  1000-410-Topologies_Merdith_et_al.gpml       topological plate polygons, 1000–410 Ma
  1000-410_poles.gpml                          paleomagnetic poles (model input data)
  250-0_plate_boundaries_Merdith_et_al.gpml    plate boundaries/topologies, 250–0 Ma
  410-250_plate_boundaries_Merdith_et_al.gpml  plate boundaries/topologies, 410–250 Ma
  TopologyBuildingBlocks_Merdith_et_al.gpml    building blocks for the topologies above
  Merdith_etal_1_Ga_reconstruction.gproj       GPlates desktop project file
  shapes_coastlines_Merdith_et_al_v2.gpmlz     coarse coastlines; the globe uses its own
                                               (more detailed) OSM land LODs instead
  shapes_cratons_Merdith_et_al.gpml            craton outlines; optional overlay, not implemented
  shapes_continents.gpml                       continental-crust layer; removed from the globe
                                               (it painted shelves and inland seas brown)

Plate boundaries and topologies are only needed to draw plate boundaries or oceanic
plates, which this globe does not show.
